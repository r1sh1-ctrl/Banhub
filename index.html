Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about BanHub.

Does BanHub directly ban accounts?

No. BanHub provides reporting and safety guidance. Account decisions are made by the relevant social media platform.

Can BanHub access my social media account?

No. Never share your password or login credentials with BanHub.

What can I use BanHub for?

You can use BanHub to understand legitimate reporting and safety options for common social media issues.
