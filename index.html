👤 Fake Account / Impersonation

Someone created a fake account using your photo, name or identity.

3× IMP — Pretending to be someone else

Then go to Other, search for your genuine account and select it when the platform asks.

2× SC4M — if the situation also involves a scam

😡 Bullying / Harassment

Someone is mentioning your ID and sending abusive or bullying messages.

3× BULLY (ME)

2× HATE — when applicable
